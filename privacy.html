Privacy

This policy covers mayurkoli.me, the personal site of Mayur Koli. It explains what the site collects, who processes it, and how to have it deleted. If you have a question about it, email kolimohit9595@gmail.com.

The short version

This site doesn't set its own cookies, doesn't run analytics, and doesn't show ads. It only receives personal information when you choose to send it: through the contact form or the chat assistant.

Contact form

When you submit the form, your name, email address, topic and message are sent to Formspree, which forwards them to my inbox. I use them only to reply to you and don't add you to any mailing list.

Chat assistant

The chat bubble on the home page is a bot running on Mentesa, a platform I built. Messages you type there are sent to the Mentesa backend (hosted on Render) to generate a reply. To keep your conversation going between page loads, the widget stores a random conversation ID in your browser's local storage. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. Please don't share sensitive personal information in the chat.

Hosting and fonts

  • The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and reliability.
  • Typefaces load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the fonts are fetched.
  • The home page reads the public RSS feed of my blog to list recent posts. That request goes from your browser to blog.mayurkoli.me.

My blog

My blog, Cellwise, lives at blog.mayurkoli.me. It shows ads served by Google AdSense, which uses cookies. That site has its own privacy policy, which describes how advertising data is handled and how to opt out of personalised ads.

Keeping and deleting your data

I keep contact-form messages in my email for as long as they're useful for our conversation. To have your messages deleted, or to ask what I hold about you, email kolimohit9595@gmail.com and I'll take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
